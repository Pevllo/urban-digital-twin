## Real Sources Used for Rates
1. **World Bank What a Waste series** (2.0 and 3.0)  
   - Egypt / MENA / upper-middle-income urban municipal solid waste generation typically 0.7–1.1 kg per capita per day.  
   - Country- and city-level aggregates published by the World Bank; used as the residential baseline.

2. **Land-use specific generation factors**  
   - Healthcare (hospitals): literature ranges ~1–5 kg/bed/day for total waste streams.  
   - Commercial / retail (mall): kg per m² GLA or per visitor from published commercial waste studies.  
   - Institutional (schools, offices): lower per-capita rates than residential, paper-heavy composition.

3. **Composition**  
   - Typical published fraction averages by generator type (organic-dominant residential; paper-dominant office; higher “other”/infectious fraction for hospitals).

## What Is Real vs Derived vs Synthetic
- **Real**: numeric base rates and composition fractions cited above.
- **Derived**: every demand and composition value in the CSV.
- **Synthetic**: all development instances and their property values (sampled to cover the What-If design space).

## No Fabricated Observations
No rows claim to be real collection-truck or weighbridge measurements of specific facilities. `data_origin = "derived"`.
